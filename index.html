Manhattan Apartments - Private vs Shared Amenities

Varun SA
MS CDP

How many apartments in Manhattan are advertised as having private amenities versus shared amenities?

Qwen (qwen3.5:2b-text) classifies apartment descriptions extracted from StreetEasy by private and shared amenities (streeteasy_full.csv ).

Private amenities

In-unit laundry, dishwashers, air conditioning, private outdoor space, or explicitly private storage, parking, gyms and pools. Ordinary kitchens, bathrooms and closets do not count.

Shared amenities

Building facilities and services: shared gardens (including vegetable gardens), communal decks, gyms, pools, lounges, laundry, elevators, parking, storage, and doormen. Paid amenities count too.

The four categories

  • 0: Neither mentioned
  • 1: Private amenities only
  • 2: Shared amenities only
  • 3: Both mentioned

Reading the maps

Neighborhood colors blend points: grey for neither, green for private, blue for shared. Both adds one green and one blue point; white means no valid listings.

Street-map dots show apartments by category.
